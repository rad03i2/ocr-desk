# Community Code of Conduct

**OCR Desk · @rad03i2**

</div>

OCR Desk welcomes constructive technical discussion and contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, documentation, or design choices.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Keep bug reports reproducible and free of private or confidential material.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time.
- Give credit when building on another contributor's work.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, or repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Project privacy

Because OCR Desk processes documents, do not publish confidential scans, credentials, personal records, or other sensitive content in issues, pull requests, discussions, or examples.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that materially violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For sensitive security matters, follow `SECURITY.md` rather than posting confidential details publicly.
